## About Sozo Wellness Center

Sozo Wellness Center integrates cutting-edge wellness technologies with holistic health principles to support whole-body healing. Rather than focusing solely on symptoms, the center seeks to identify and address underlying imbalances that may be impacting a person's overall well-being. 

The name "Sozo" reflects a philosophy of complete healing and restoration of the mind, body, and spirit. The center's approach is centered on helping clients achieve optimal wellness through personalized assessments, targeted therapies, and ongoing support. 

## Mission

To provide a comprehensive, root-cause approach to health and wellness by empowering individuals with personalized assessments, innovative therapies, and holistic solutions that support lasting transformation.
